Privacy Policy

In short: no account, no personal data collected, your chats stay in your own browser.

Last updated: November 2026

On this page

    1. Data controller and nature of the project

    CodeVocab is designed by the developer whyasaf as a portfolio project. Its main goal is to modernise language learning with AI. It has no commercial data-processing purpose.

    2. No processing of personal data

    CodeVocab does not ask for or collect directly identifying data such as your name, email address or phone number. There is no sign-up.

    To prevent abuse of the free AI quotas, the requesting IP address and the time of the request are kept only in server memory for at most 1 hour. This information is never written to a database or file, is not used for anything else, and is deleted automatically when the time is up.

    A cookie named cv_app remembers that you have opened the app before. It does not identify you; it is only used to take you straight to the app when you visit the home page. A cookie named cv_lang remembers your language choice.

    3. Local storage

    Your chat history, learned words and theme preference are never sent to our servers. They are stored only on your device, in your browser's "LocalStorage".

    • You are in control: you can delete a chat from the sidebar, or remove all your progress by clearing your browser data. The developer has no access to this data.

    4. AI and API integration

    The text you write for checking and the words you look up are sent in real time to one of the third-party AI providers (Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter). When you continue a chat, the last few messages of the same chat are included for context. The CodeVocab server does not store this text.

    Important: the privacy of text shared with the AI is subject to the provider's own data policies. Never type passwords, private conversations or sensitive personal data into the app.

    5. Cookies

    CodeVocab does not use third-party cookies for advertising or tracking. Only local storage and the two functional cookies described above (cv_app and cv_lang) are used.

    6. Contact

    For questions or feedback about this policy, you can reach the developer via the project's GitHub page or at whyasaf.com.
