User guide

CodeVocab in five minutes.

One chat screen, three modes. Everything you write feels like talking to a mentor.

On this page

    Why CodeVocab?

    It's designed to help you learn English through real usage, not just dictionary meanings. It focuses on the traps learners fall into in messages, emails and everyday conversation: translating word for word from your own language, the wrong tense, an unnecessary preposition.

    Sentence check

    With Check mode selected in the input box, write an English sentence and send it.

    1. ScoreYour sentence is scored out of 100. A correct, natural sentence always scores 100.
    2. Correct versionThe corrected sentence is shown; removed words are struck through, added ones are green. You can listen to it or copy it.
    3. Mentor's noteA short paragraph explains why it was wrong and what the right rule is.
    95–100Excellent
    80–94Very good
    60–79Needs work
    0–59Try again

    Ask the mentor

    After a check, you can keep chatting. Tap a ready-made question like "Why is that?" or "How do I say it more formally?", or write your own. The mentor remembers the earlier messages in the same chat.

    Example: when do we use the present perfect? or how can I say this more politely?

    Word mode

    Switch to the Word tab in the input box.

    • Random: you get a pick of 5 words from the pool; tap one.
    • Your own word: type the word you're curious about (e.g. awkward) and send it.
    • Card: meaning, IPA pronunciation, read-aloud with the speaker icon, A1/B1/C1 example sentences and a tip about the most common mistake.
    • Make a sentence: the button on the card switches to Check mode so you can try the word yourself.

    Study a topic

    Switch to the Topic tab and type a grammar topic or pattern (e.g. present perfect, used to, prepositions). Typos are fine; the topic name is normalised automatically.

    1. Lesson cardSummary, formula, when it's used (with real-life examples), a comparison with Turkish, common traps and signal words.
    2. Mini quiz5 questions: multiple choice and fill in the blank. Your answer is checked instantly with an explanation.
    3. ResultYour score is saved. Use New questions to try again on the same topic, or Ask a question if you're stuck.

    Pattern of the day: the first suggestion in Topic mode changes every day. It's the easiest way to study one pattern a day.

    Every lesson is written by one AI and checked by a second one before you see it. Quizzes can still occasionally be wrong. If you think your answer is also correct, tap Ask the mentor under the question; the mentor will judge it fairly.

    Learned and history

    • A word you mark with I know this won't appear in random picks again.
    • The Learned window at the bottom left shows your learned words and studied topics with your best scores.
    • Every chat is saved by date in the sidebar; use the search box to find old chats. Rename a chat with the pencil icon or by double-clicking it.

    This data lives only in your browser's LocalStorage. If you clear your browser data, your progress is deleted too.

    Install as an app

    Tap Install app at the bottom of the sidebar to add CodeVocab to your home screen or desktop. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap the Share icon and choose Add to Home Screen.

    Shortcuts

    • Enter send, Shift + Enter new line
    • ⌘ / Ctrl + K new chat
    • Esc close the menu on mobile

    Tips

    • Consistency: try to add at least 5 words a day to your "I know this" list.
    • Say it out loud: listen to the corrected sentence with the speaker icon and repeat it.
    • Real sentences: check the messages you actually write; that's what sticks the most.
    • On the go: it works on your phone too, so you can keep going on the bus or during a coffee break.
