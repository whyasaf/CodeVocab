About

Born from one question: "Did I write this right?"

CodeVocab is the first software project of Ömer Asaf Ak (whyasaf). It started as a simple vocabulary app; today it's a free AI mentor for everyone learning English.

Why?

To end the doubt.

The most tiring moment in learning English is writing a sentence and not being sure it's right. Translation tools change your sentence but never say why. Word lists make you memorise but don't teach you to use.

CodeVocab has one job: if your sentence is wrong, explain why; if it's right, leave it alone.

The journey

Three versions, one year.

  1. February 2026

    v1 · First step

    The first version goes live with a 3,000-word pool and Gemini. Word cards and a simple sentence check.

  2. April 2026

    v2 · Faster AI

    Switched to Groq when the free quotas ran out too fast. The mentor prompt was rewritten and checks got sharper.

  3. November 2026

    v3 · A mentor you can chat with

    A chat interface with history, a "Study a topic" mode with quizzes, a free AI chain with automatic fallback and a brand-new design.

Principles

Three things that won't change.

It stays free

No sign-up, no subscription, no ads. It runs entirely on free AI tiers.

Your data, your device

Your chats and progress are stored in your own browser, not on a server.

Don't touch what's right

A correct, natural sentence always scores 100. The mentor never suggests needless "improvements".

Technology

The engine behind it.

If one AI provider runs out of quota, the request moves to the next one automatically; you won't notice a thing.

AI chain

Models on Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter, with automatic fallback.

Flask Python

A light, fast backend running serverless on Vercel.

Vanilla JS

No framework. The UI is plain JavaScript and modern CSS.

SQLite

A light, portable, read-only database for the 2,700+ word pool.
